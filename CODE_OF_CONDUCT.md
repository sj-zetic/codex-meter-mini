# Code of Conduct

We want Codex Meter to be a welcoming, constructive project.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, patient, and specific.
- Give actionable technical feedback and assume good intent.
- Respect privacy and never post another person's credentials or account data.
- Welcome contributors regardless of experience or background.
- Keep discussions focused on improving the project.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sustained disruption, personal attacks, doxxing, and publishing private information are not tolerated.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner through their GitHub profile. Maintainers may edit or remove contributions, lock conversations, or temporarily or permanently restrict participation when necessary. Enforcement decisions should be proportionate, consistent, and respectful of reporter privacy.
